Back to the plan

Every day · 5 minutes · no kit

Desk reset

Once in the morning and once mid-afternoon, right at your desk. On long days set a timer for every 90 minutes, stand up and do any two. Little and often beats one big stretch on a Sunday, every time.

1 · Chin tucks

10 reps, 3-sec hold

Sit tall and glide your head straight back, as if pulling a double chin, eyes level. Hold for three seconds. Undoes the forward-poking head that screens give everyone.

2 · Chair-back extension

8 slow reps

Hands behind your head, elbows forward. Lean your upper back over the top of the chair and look up at the ceiling, bum staying on the seat. Breathe out on the way back. Works best on a chair with a lower back.

3 · Ankle circles + calf raises

10 circles each way, 15 raises

Seated, draw slow circles with each foot. Then stand, hold the desk and rise up onto your toes for two seconds, lowering for three. This is the one for the clicky ankles and knees.

4 · Standing hip flexor stretch

30 s each side

Step one foot back, back heel lifted. Tuck your tailbone under and squeeze the glute of the back leg until you feel a stretch across the front of that hip. Sitting all day shortens these muscles more than any others.

Before bed

Five rounds of the 90/90 breathing from Day C with your heels up on the sofa or bed. It's the easiest way to undo a day of shallow desk breathing.
