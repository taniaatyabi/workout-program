Day B · Wednesday · about 60 min

Upper body & posture

Pulls your shoulders back from the keyboard. There are twice as many pulling moves as pushing ones today, on purpose: your chest is already tight from sitting, and your upper back is the bit that's been asleep.

Rowing machine Lat pulldown Seated row Adjustable bench + dumbbells Cable machine + rope Mat
Warm-up · 8 min

Rowing machine at an easy rhythm for 6 minutes. Then 10 cat-cows on a mat and 10 band pull-aparts with a resistance band from the functional area.

B1

Lat pulldown

Kit · Lat pulldown machine, long bar
Wk 1–4 2 × 12 Wk 5–8 3 × 10 Wk 9–12 3 × 8
  1. 1Set the thigh pad snug on your legs. Grip the bar a little wider than your shoulders and sit tall, arms long.
  2. 2Pull your shoulder blades down first, then bring the bar to your upper chest, elbows driving down towards your back pockets.
  3. 3Let the bar rise slowly until your arms are straight again.

Watch out: a slight lean back is fine. Swinging your whole body to get the bar down means it's too heavy.

B2

Seated cable row

Kit · Seated row machine, close-grip handle
Wk 1–4 2 × 12 Wk 5–8 3 × 10 Wk 9–12 3 × 8
  1. 1Feet on the plate, knees slightly bent. Sit tall holding the handle with your arms straight.
  2. 2Pull the handle to your belly button and squeeze your shoulder blades together, like pinching a pencil between them.
  3. 3Let your arms straighten slowly without rounding forward.

Watch out: your torso stays still. Rocking back to move the weight means it's too heavy.

B3

Incline dumbbell press

Kit · Adjustable bench at a low incline + dumbbells
Wk 1–4 2 × 10 Wk 5–8 3 × 10 Wk 9–12 3 × 8
  1. 1Set the bench one or two notches up from flat. Sit with the dumbbells on your thighs, lie back and bring them to chest level.
  2. 2Press up over your shoulders until your arms are nearly straight.
  3. 3Lower slowly with your elbows angled about 45° from your sides, not flared out wide.

Watch out: feet flat on the floor and shoulder blades pinned back into the bench. This is the move where the wobble shows up first, which is normal.

B4

Cable face pull

Kit · Multi-pulley cable machine, rope attachment
Wk 1–4 2 × 15 Wk 5–8 3 × 15 Wk 9–12 3 × 15, 2-sec hold
  1. 1Set the pulley just above head height with the rope on. Hold an end in each hand and step back until your arms are straight.
  2. 2Pull the rope towards your forehead, splitting the ends apart, elbows high and wide.
  3. 3Pause, squeeze the backs of your shoulders, then let it out slowly.

Keep it light: perfect control over weight, every time. If one exercise in this plan undoes desk posture, it's this one.

B5 · Core

Bird dog

Kit · Mat
Wk 1–4 2 × 6 each side Wk 5–8 3 × 8 Wk 9–12 3 × 10
  1. 1Hands under shoulders, knees under hips, back flat like a tabletop.
  2. 2Reach one arm forward and the opposite leg back until both are level with your body.
  3. 3Hold for two seconds, come back under control and swap sides.

Picture this: a glass of water balanced on your lower back. Don't spill it.
