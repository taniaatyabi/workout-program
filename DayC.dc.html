Day C · Friday · about 60 min

Full body & core

The control day. It opens with a breathing drill that puts your ribs and pelvis back where they belong, then moves to one-leg work for your knees and ankles and core exercises that train you to resist twisting and sagging.

Stair climber Bench or plyo box Dumbbells Cable machine Mat Cross trainer
Warm-up · 8 min

Stair climber at a slow, steady step for 6 minutes (or the cross trainer). Then go straight into C1 on a mat.

C1 · Posture

90/90 breathing

Kit · Mat + flat bench
Every week 2 rounds of 5 slow breaths
  1. 1Lie on your back with your heels resting on a bench, hips and knees both bent at 90°.
  2. 2Breathe in through your nose for 4. Breathe out through your mouth for 6 to 8, letting your ribs sink down towards the floor.
  3. 3On each exhale, gently dig your heels into the bench until the backs of your thighs switch on and your tailbone lifts a few centimetres.

Feels like nothing? Good. It resets how your ribs and pelvis sit before you load anything. Worth doing at home before bed too.

C2

Glute bridge → hip thrust

Kit · Mat, then bench + dumbbell from week 5
Wk 1–4 2 × 12 bridge Wk 5–8 3 × 10 hip thrust Wk 9–12 3 × 8, barbell + pad
  1. 1Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat and hip-width apart, arms by your sides.
  2. 2Breathe out, press through your heels and lift your hips until knees, hips and shoulders make a straight line.
  3. 3Squeeze your glutes hard for two seconds at the top, then lower slowly.

Feeling it in your lower back or hamstrings? Walk your feet a little closer and tuck your ribs down. From week 5, rest your upper back against the side of a bench and hold a dumbbell on your hips.

C3

Dumbbell step-up

Kit · Bench or plyo box (functional area) + dumbbells
Wk 1–4 2 × 8 each leg Wk 5–8 3 × 8 Wk 9–12 3 × 10
  1. 1Face a bench or box around knee height, dumbbells at your sides. Put your whole foot on top.
  2. 2Push through that front heel and stand up tall on the box. Try not to bounce off your back foot.
  3. 3Step back down slowly. Do every rep on one leg, then switch.

Knee wobbling? Drop the dumbbells and use a lower box until it steadies. A wobble that settles over the weeks is your ankle and knee getting stronger.

C4

Seated shoulder press

Kit · Adjustable bench set upright + dumbbells
Wk 1–4 2 × 10 Wk 5–8 3 × 10 Wk 9–12 3 × 8
  1. 1Sit with your back against the upright bench. Dumbbells at shoulder height, palms facing forward, elbows under your wrists.
  2. 2Press up until your arms are nearly straight overhead.
  3. 3Lower slowly back to ear height.

Watch out: keep your ribs down and your back on the pad. Arching to finish the rep means the weight's too heavy.

C5 · Core

Pallof press

Kit · Cable machine, single handle at chest height
Wk 1–4 2 × 8 each side Wk 5–8 3 × 10 Wk 9–12 3 × 10, 3-sec hold
  1. 1Stand side-on to the machine, feet hip-width, holding the handle in both hands against your breastbone. Step out until the cable has tension.
  2. 2Press the handle straight out in front of you. The cable will try to twist you towards the machine. Don't let it.
  3. 3Hold for two seconds, bring it back in. Finish the set, then turn round and face the other way.

The point: nothing moves except your arms. Squeeze your glutes and keep your hips square. The harder it is to stay still, the harder your core is working.

C6 · Core

Side plank

Kit · Mat
Wk 1–4 2 × 15 s each side Wk 5–8 3 × 20 s Wk 9–12 3 × 30 s
  1. 1Lie on your side with your elbow under your shoulder and your legs stacked.
  2. 2Lift your hips until your body is one straight line from head to feet.
  3. 3Breathe steadily and hold. Lower with control and swap sides.

Easier version: bend both knees and balance on your knees instead of your feet. Use it whenever your hips start to sag.
