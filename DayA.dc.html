Day A · Monday · about 60 min

Lower body & glutes

Wakes up the glutes and hamstrings that sitting switches off, and builds the muscle that steadies your knees and ankles. If you only manage one session in a bad week, make it this one.

Cross trainer Leg press Dumbbells + bench Outer thigh machine Mat Treadmill
Warm-up · 8 min

Cross trainer at an easy pace where you could chat. Then 10 bodyweight sit-to-stands from a bench and 10 glute bridges on a mat.

A1

Leg press

Kit · Plate-loaded leg press
Wk 1–4 2 × 12 Wk 5–8 3 × 10 Wk 9–12 3 × 8
  1. 1Sit with your lower back flat on the pad. Feet hip-width in the middle of the platform, knees bent towards your chest.
  2. 2Push through your whole foot until your legs are almost straight. Keep a soft bend; never snap the knees locked.
  3. 3Lower for a slow count of three until your knees are back at roughly 90°.

Watch out: if your lower back peels off the pad at the bottom, you're going too deep. Stop a little earlier.

A2

Dumbbell Romanian deadlift

Kit · Pair of dumbbells
Wk 1–4 2 × 12 Wk 5–8 3 × 10 Wk 9–12 3 × 8, barbell optional
  1. 1Stand tall with the dumbbells resting on the front of your thighs, knees softly bent.
  2. 2Push your hips straight back, like shutting a car door with your bum. Let the weights slide down your legs to mid-shin.
  3. 3Feel the stretch in the back of your thighs, then drive your hips forward to stand. Squeeze your glutes at the top.

Watch out: your back stays flat the whole way. The bend comes from your hips. If your back starts to round, you've gone low enough.

A3

Goblet box squat

Kit · One dumbbell + flat bench
Wk 1–4 2 × 10 Wk 5–8 3 × 10 Wk 9–12 3 × 8 in the squat rack
  1. 1Stand a foot in front of a flat bench. Hold one dumbbell upright against your chest, elbows tucked in.
  2. 2Sit back and down until your bum lightly taps the bench. Tap, don't sit and relax.
  3. 3Drive up through your heels with your knees pointing over your toes.

Watch out: knees caving in means the weight's too heavy. Think about spreading the floor apart with your feet. From week 9, do these with a barbell in the squat rack, safeties set just below bench height.

A4

Outer thigh machine

Kit · Inner / outer thigh machine, pads outside
Wk 1–4 2 × 15 Wk 5–8 3 × 15 Wk 9–12 3 × 12
  1. 1Sit tall with the pads on the outside of your knees and hold the handles.
  2. 2Push your knees out as wide as feels comfortable and pause for a second.
  3. 3Bring them back slowly. The weight stack shouldn't clank.

Why bother: the side glute muscles keep your knees from drifting inwards on stairs and squats. Lean your chest slightly forward to feel them more.

A5 · Core

Dead bug

Kit · Mat (functional area or studio)
Wk 1–4 2 × 6 each side Wk 5–8 3 × 8 Wk 9–12 3 × 10
  1. 1Lie on your back, arms pointing at the ceiling, knees bent at 90° over your hips.
  2. 2Breathe out and press your lower back into the mat. Slowly reach one arm overhead and the opposite leg out long.
  3. 3Come back and switch sides. Only go as low as you can with your back still flat.

Watch out: slow beats fast here. If your back arches off the mat, your ribs have popped up. Breathe out harder and shorten the reach.
